First steps with WalletFeed

WalletFeed shows the transactions from your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings accounts on your iPhone. To start, you open WalletFeed and select the Wallet accounts that it can read.

Before you start

You must have these items:

  • An iPhone with iOS 26 or a newer version. WalletFeed is available only in the US.
  • An Apple Card, Apple Cash or Savings account in Wallet on the iPhone.
  • WalletFeed on the iPhone. You can get WalletFeed from the App Store.

If you do not have one of these accounts, refer to Use WalletFeed with sample transactions.

Open WalletFeed for the first time

  1. On your iPhone, open WalletFeed.

    The Welcome to WalletFeed screen opens. It tells you about Ask Spending, Destinations, On-Device Privacy and Background Sync.

    If Apple Intelligence is not available on your iPhone, the first item is Spending and Search.

  2. Tap Continue.

    Transactions opens, and iOS shows a message with the buttons Don't Allow and Select Accounts.

Select the Wallet accounts for WalletFeed

After you share accounts or tap Don't Allow, iOS does not show this message again. Then you can change the accounts that you share only in the Settings app.

  1. Tap Select Accounts.

    The list of Wallet accounts opens.

  2. Select each account for WalletFeed, for example your Apple Card.

    You can also select a start date for each account. WalletFeed can read only the transactions that occur on or after that date.

  3. Tap the Share with button.

    Transactions shows the transactions from the accounts that you selected.

If you tap Don't Allow, Transactions shows Wallet Access Off. To let WalletFeed read Wallet after that, refer to If Transactions shows "Wallet Access Off".

If you tap Cancel in the list of Wallet accounts, Transactions shows No Cards. To select the accounts again, refer to If Transactions shows "No Cards".

Note: WalletFeed reads your transactions from Wallet on your iPhone. It does not keep the transactions. WalletFeed has no account and no WalletFeed servers.

See your transactions

Transactions shows your transactions below the name of each month. The newest transactions are at the top.

Each row shows the merchant and the amount. The day is below the amount. Below the merchant, a row can also show "Pending". If a transaction is not from the account that has the most transactions, its row also shows the account name, for example Apple Cash.

At the end of the list, WalletFeed shows the date of the first transaction that it can read. To see transactions from before that date, refer to Change the accounts that you share.

Use the other tabs

The tabs and the search button at the bottom of the screen open the other parts of WalletFeed:

  • The Spending tab shows the total of your purchases this month, for each day, category and merchant. Refer to Spending.
  • The Destinations tab shows where WalletFeed sends your transactions, for example to a budget app or a spreadsheet. Refer to Destinations.
  • The search button opens Search, which finds the transactions that have the merchant or the amount that you type. Refer to Search.

You can use Spending, Ask Spending, Search, Recurring Charges, the Spending widget and CSV export without WalletFeed Pro. To send transactions to a destination, you must have WalletFeed Pro. WalletFeed Pro is a subscription with a 1-week free trial, or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

Use WalletFeed with sample transactions

If you do not have an Apple Card, Apple Cash or Savings account, you can use WalletFeed with sample transactions. WalletFeed shows the Sample Data switch only if you have no destinations or if the switch is on.

  1. In WalletFeed, tap Settings (the gear button) at the top of the screen.

  2. In the Data section, set the Sample Data switch to on.

  3. Tap Done.

    Transactions shows two months of sample transactions. It does not show the transactions from your Wallet.

When you set the Sample Data switch to off, WalletFeed shows the transactions from your Wallet again.

Correct problems with Wallet accounts

If Transactions shows "Wallet Access Off"

WalletFeed cannot read your Wallet accounts. This problem occurs after you tap Don't Allow, or after you remove the Wallet access for WalletFeed in the Settings app.

  1. On your iPhone, open the Settings app.

  2. Tap Privacy & Security › Wallet › WalletFeed.

  3. Select the accounts that WalletFeed can read.

  4. Open WalletFeed again.

    Transactions shows your transactions.

If Transactions shows "No Cards"

You did not share Wallet accounts with WalletFeed, and you did not tap Don't Allow. This problem occurs after you tap Cancel in the list of Wallet accounts.

  1. In Transactions, tap Choose Accounts.
  2. Do the steps in Select the Wallet accounts for WalletFeed.

If Transactions shows "No Transactions"

WalletFeed can read Wallet, but it finds no transactions in the accounts that you selected.

  1. On your iPhone, open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Privacy & Security › Wallet › WalletFeed.
  3. Select more Wallet accounts for WalletFeed.
  4. Open WalletFeed again.

If Transactions shows "Wallet Unavailable"

On this iPhone, WalletFeed cannot read transactions from Wallet. Below Wallet Unavailable, WalletFeed shows "This iPhone can't share Wallet data." To use WalletFeed without Wallet data, refer to Use WalletFeed with sample transactions.
