WalletFeed User Guide

How to use each part of WalletFeed on your iPhone.

  • First steps with WalletFeedOpen WalletFeed on your iPhone for the first time and select the Wallet accounts that it can read. Then look at your Apple Card transactions.
  • Select the Wallet accounts that WalletFeed readsGive WalletFeed access to your Apple Card, Apple Cash or Savings account on your iPhone. Change the accounts and their start dates in the Settings app.
  • TransactionsWalletFeed shows your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions on your iPhone. The list shows pending charges and refunds in month sections.
  • SpendingSpending in WalletFeed shows the total of your Apple Card and Apple Cash purchases this month. It also shows the totals by day, category and merchant.
  • Ask Spending with Apple IntelligenceIn Ask Spending, you type text about your Apple Card transactions, for example "Dining this month vs last". WalletFeed shows a chart, a list and a summary.
  • SearchFind your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions with Search in WalletFeed. Type a merchant name or an amount, or use Apple Intelligence.
  • Recurring Charges and alertsWalletFeed finds the recurring charges on your Apple Card, such as subscriptions. It can send a notification for a new recurring charge or a price change.
  • Spending widgetPut the WalletFeed Spending widget on your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows the This Month total and the Same Point Last Month total.
  • Make a CSV file with Export CSVMake a CSV file of your Apple Card transactions on your iPhone with Export CSV in WalletFeed. You select a period, then you save or send the file.
  • Siri and ShortcutsUse Siri and the Shortcuts app with WalletFeed on your iPhone to open Spending, find transactions and send Apple Card transactions to destinations.
  • WalletFeed ProWalletFeed Pro lets WalletFeed send your Apple Card transactions to destinations. This page gives the prices and tells you how to change or cancel a plan.
  • DestinationsWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to an app, a spreadsheet or a file. You can add, change, stop and remove destinations.
  • Filter the transactions for a destinationA WalletFeed filter selects the Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions that a destination receives, by account, category, merchant or amount.
  • Background SyncBackground Sync sends your Apple Card transactions to your destinations while WalletFeed is not open. Automations in Shortcuts can also send them.
  • Send previous transactionsSend History sends your previous Apple Card transactions to a WalletFeed destination. Initial History sets the start date of new destinations.
  • Privacy and securityThis page about WalletFeed privacy and security tells you which data WalletFeed keeps and sends, and how to lock the app with Face ID.
  • TroubleshootingThe steps on this page correct problems with WalletFeed, for example missing cards, "Authentication failed", "Sync Stopped" and copies of transactions.
  • Webhook formatThe JSON a webhook destination receives, and how to verify its signature.
