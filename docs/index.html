WalletFeed User Guide

How to use each part of WalletFeed on your iPhone.

  • First steps with WalletFeedOpen WalletFeed on your iPhone for the first time and select the Wallet accounts that it can read. Then see your Apple Card transactions.
  • Select the Wallet accounts that WalletFeed readsShare your Apple Card, Apple Cash or Savings account with WalletFeed on your iPhone. Change the accounts and their start dates in the Settings app.
  • TransactionsSee your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions by month in WalletFeed on your iPhone, with pending transactions and refunds.
  • SpendingSpending in WalletFeed shows the total of your Apple Card and Apple Cash purchases this month on your iPhone, by day, category and merchant.
  • Ask Spending with Apple IntelligenceType a question about your Apple Card spending on iPhone, for example "Dining this month vs last". WalletFeed shows a chart, a list and a summary.
  • SearchFind your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions with Search in WalletFeed. Type a merchant, a category or an amount, or use Apple Intelligence.
  • Recurring Charges and alertsWalletFeed finds the recurring charges on your Apple Card, such as subscriptions, and can send a notification when a new one starts or a price changes.
  • Spending widgetPut the WalletFeed Spending widget on your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows this month's total and last month's total to the same day and time.
  • Export a CSV fileExport your Apple Card transactions to a CSV file on your iPhone with Export CSV in WalletFeed. You select a period, then save or send the file.
  • Siri and ShortcutsUse Siri and the Shortcuts app with WalletFeed on your iPhone to open Spending, find transactions and send Apple Card transactions to destinations.
  • WalletFeed ProWalletFeed Pro lets WalletFeed send your Apple Card transactions to destinations. This page gives the prices and tells you how to change or cancel a plan.
  • DestinationsSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to an app, a spreadsheet or a file with WalletFeed. Add, test, change or remove a destination.
  • Choose what a destination receivesSelect which of your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions a WalletFeed destination receives, by account, category, merchant or amount.
  • Background SyncSend your Apple Card transactions to your destinations while WalletFeed is closed, with Background Sync and automations in Shortcuts on your iPhone.
  • Send previous transactionsSend your previous Apple Card transactions to a WalletFeed destination with Send History, and set the start date of new destinations with Initial History.
  • Privacy and securityWalletFeed privacy and security on iPhone: the data that WalletFeed keeps, the data that it sends, and how to lock the app with Face ID.
  • TroubleshootingCorrect problems with WalletFeed: missing cards, Ask Spending, Authentication failed, a sync that stops, and copies of transactions.
  • Webhook formatThe JSON a webhook destination receives, and how to verify its signature.
