# WalletFeed User Guide

How to use each part of WalletFeed on your iPhone.

- [First steps with WalletFeed](https://walletfeed.app/docs/get-started/index.md): Open WalletFeed on your iPhone for the first time and select the Wallet accounts that it can read. Then see your Apple Card transactions.
- [Select the Wallet accounts that WalletFeed reads](https://walletfeed.app/docs/wallet-accounts/index.md): Share your Apple Card, Apple Cash or Savings account with WalletFeed on your iPhone. Change the accounts and their start dates in the Settings app.
- [Transactions](https://walletfeed.app/docs/transactions/index.md): See your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions by month in WalletFeed on your iPhone, with pending transactions and refunds.
- [Spending](https://walletfeed.app/docs/spending/index.md): Spending in WalletFeed shows the total of your Apple Card and Apple Cash purchases this month on your iPhone, by day, category and merchant.
- [Ask Spending with Apple Intelligence](https://walletfeed.app/docs/ask-spending/index.md): Type a question about your Apple Card spending on iPhone, for example "Dining this month vs last". WalletFeed shows a chart, a list and a summary.
- [Search](https://walletfeed.app/docs/search/index.md): Find your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions with Search in WalletFeed. Type a merchant, a category or an amount, or use Apple Intelligence.
- [Recurring Charges and alerts](https://walletfeed.app/docs/recurring-charges/index.md): WalletFeed finds the recurring charges on your Apple Card, such as subscriptions, and can send a notification when a new one starts or a price changes.
- [Spending widget](https://walletfeed.app/docs/spending-widget/index.md): Put the WalletFeed Spending widget on your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows this month's total and last month's total to the same day and time.
- [Export a CSV file](https://walletfeed.app/docs/export-csv/index.md): Export your Apple Card transactions to a CSV file on your iPhone with Export CSV in WalletFeed. You select a period, then save or send the file.
- [Siri and Shortcuts](https://walletfeed.app/docs/siri-and-shortcuts/index.md): Use Siri and the Shortcuts app with WalletFeed on your iPhone to open Spending, find transactions and send Apple Card transactions to destinations.
- [WalletFeed Pro](https://walletfeed.app/docs/walletfeed-pro/index.md): WalletFeed Pro lets WalletFeed send your Apple Card transactions to destinations. This page gives the prices and tells you how to change or cancel a plan.
- [Destinations](https://walletfeed.app/docs/destinations/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to an app, a spreadsheet or a file with WalletFeed. Add, test, change or remove a destination.
- [Choose what a destination receives](https://walletfeed.app/docs/choose-what-to-send/index.md): Select which of your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions a WalletFeed destination receives, by account, category, merchant or amount.
- [Background Sync](https://walletfeed.app/docs/background-sync/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions to your destinations while WalletFeed is closed, with Background Sync and automations in Shortcuts on your iPhone.
- [Send previous transactions](https://walletfeed.app/docs/send-history/index.md): Send your previous Apple Card transactions to a WalletFeed destination with Send History, and set the start date of new destinations with Initial History.
- [Privacy and security](https://walletfeed.app/docs/privacy-and-security/index.md): WalletFeed privacy and security on iPhone: the data that WalletFeed keeps, the data that it sends, and how to lock the app with Face ID.
- [Troubleshooting](https://walletfeed.app/docs/troubleshooting/index.md): Correct problems with WalletFeed: missing cards, Ask Spending, Authentication failed, a sync that stops, and copies of transactions.
