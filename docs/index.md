# WalletFeed User Guide

How to use each part of WalletFeed on your iPhone.

- [First steps with WalletFeed](https://walletfeed.app/docs/get-started/index.md): Open WalletFeed on your iPhone for the first time and select the Wallet accounts that it can read. Then look at your Apple Card transactions.
- [Select the Wallet accounts that WalletFeed reads](https://walletfeed.app/docs/wallet-accounts/index.md): Give WalletFeed access to your Apple Card, Apple Cash or Savings account on your iPhone. Change the accounts and their start dates in the Settings app.
- [Transactions](https://walletfeed.app/docs/transactions/index.md): WalletFeed shows your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions on your iPhone. The list shows pending charges and refunds in month sections.
- [Spending](https://walletfeed.app/docs/spending/index.md): Spending in WalletFeed shows the total of your Apple Card and Apple Cash purchases this month. It also shows the totals by day, category and merchant.
- [Ask Spending with Apple Intelligence](https://walletfeed.app/docs/ask-spending/index.md): In Ask Spending, you type text about your Apple Card transactions, for example "Dining this month vs last". WalletFeed shows a chart, a list and a summary.
- [Search](https://walletfeed.app/docs/search/index.md): Find your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions with Search in WalletFeed. Type a merchant name or an amount, or use Apple Intelligence.
- [Recurring Charges and alerts](https://walletfeed.app/docs/recurring-charges/index.md): WalletFeed finds the recurring charges on your Apple Card, such as subscriptions. It can send a notification for a new recurring charge or a price change.
- [Spending widget](https://walletfeed.app/docs/spending-widget/index.md): Put the WalletFeed Spending widget on your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows the This Month total and the Same Point Last Month total.
- [Make a CSV file with Export CSV](https://walletfeed.app/docs/export-csv/index.md): Make a CSV file of your Apple Card transactions on your iPhone with Export CSV in WalletFeed. You select a period, then you save or send the file.
- [Siri and Shortcuts](https://walletfeed.app/docs/siri-and-shortcuts/index.md): Use Siri and the Shortcuts app with WalletFeed on your iPhone to open Spending, find transactions and send Apple Card transactions to destinations.
- [WalletFeed Pro](https://walletfeed.app/docs/walletfeed-pro/index.md): WalletFeed Pro lets WalletFeed send your Apple Card transactions to destinations. This page gives the prices and tells you how to change or cancel a plan.
- [Destinations](https://walletfeed.app/docs/destinations/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to an app, a spreadsheet or a file. You can add, change, stop and remove destinations.
- [Filter the transactions for a destination](https://walletfeed.app/docs/choose-what-to-send/index.md): A WalletFeed filter selects the Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions that a destination receives, by account, category, merchant or amount.
- [Background Sync](https://walletfeed.app/docs/background-sync/index.md): Background Sync sends your Apple Card transactions to your destinations while WalletFeed is not open. Automations in Shortcuts can also send them.
- [Send previous transactions](https://walletfeed.app/docs/send-history/index.md): Send History sends your previous Apple Card transactions to a WalletFeed destination. Initial History sets the start date of new destinations.
- [Privacy and security](https://walletfeed.app/docs/privacy-and-security/index.md): This page about WalletFeed privacy and security tells you which data WalletFeed keeps and sends, and how to lock the app with Face ID.
- [Troubleshooting](https://walletfeed.app/docs/troubleshooting/index.md): The steps on this page correct problems with WalletFeed, for example missing cards, "Authentication failed", "Sync Stopped" and copies of transactions.
