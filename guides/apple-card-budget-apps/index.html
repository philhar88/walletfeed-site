Budgeting apps that support Apple Card and Apple Cash

These budgeting apps support Apple Card on iPhone: YNAB, Monarch, Copilot, PocketGuard, Rocket Money, Quicken Simplifi and Origin. The help pages of YNAB, Monarch, Copilot and PocketGuard also give Apple Cash and Savings. The Origin help pages also give Apple Cash.

Download WalletFeed on the App Store

Apple Card does not connect to an app with your login, as a bank does. An app can get your Apple Card transactions in three ways:

  • The app reads Wallet on your iPhone.
  • WalletFeed sends the transactions to the app from your iPhone.
  • You import a file into the app.

Apps that connect to Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings

On November 26, 2026, the help pages of these apps gave these Wallet accounts. "Not stated" tells you that the help page does not give the account. You start each connection in the app on your iPhone.

App Apple Card Apple Cash Savings Conditions on the help page
YNAB Yes Yes Yes iOS 17.4 or a newer version. YNAB then shows the transactions in its web app and its Android app.
Monarch Yes Yes Yes The newest iOS version, and the Monarch app on an iPhone.
Copilot Yes Yes Yes iOS 17.4 or a newer version. Copilot then shows the accounts in its Mac app and its iPad app.
PocketGuard Yes Yes Yes iOS 17.4 or a newer version, and Background App Refresh for PocketGuard.
Rocket Money Yes Not stated Not stated iOS 17.4 or a newer version. The connection goes through Plaid.
Quicken Simplifi Yes Not stated Not stated The Quicken Simplifi app on an iPhone. Quicken Simplifi then shows the accounts in its web app.
Origin Yes Yes Not stated The device that made the connection, and All Available Activity in the Wallet settings for Origin.

The Quicken Simplifi page gives "Apple Wallet accounts, including your Apple Card".

Apple gives Wallet transactions to iPhone apps through FinanceKit, its software for financial apps. Apple's FinanceKit page for software developers gives these conditions for the US:

  • An iPhone with iOS 17.4 or a newer version
  • Apple Card, but not for a participant in Apple Card Family
  • Apple Cash, but not for a child in Apple Cash Family
  • Savings.

Does Plaid support Apple Card? tells you how these connections operate, and gives the steps for Rocket Money.

Apps that do not connect to Apple Card

On November 26, 2026, the help pages of these apps gave these statements:

App Statement on the help page Files that the app can import
Tiller "The connection to Apple Card is not supported by our data provider." A CSV file, in the Tiller Console
EveryDollar "EveryDollar currently does not support a connection with Apple Card." No files

The Tiller page gives a procedure for Apple Card. You add a manual account in the Tiller Console. Then you import a CSV file from Wallet.

Apps that WalletFeed sends Apple Card to

WalletFeed reads your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from Wallet on your iPhone. With WalletFeed Pro, WalletFeed sends each new or changed transaction to a destination. Each guide gives the steps:

Type Destinations
Budget apps YNAB, Lunch Money, PocketSmith, Toshl Finance, Firefly III, Sure, Wallet by BudgetBakers
Accounts software QuickBooks Online, Xero
Spreadsheets Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, Coda
Automations Zapier, Make, a webhook

WalletFeed is available only in the US, for an iPhone with iOS 26 or a newer version. WalletFeed has no account, and no WalletFeed server keeps your transactions.

Apps that import a file

Some apps cannot connect to Apple Card, but they can import a file. WalletFeed writes your transactions to a file in a folder that you select, and updates the file.

App File Guide
Quicken for Mac OFX Import Apple Card transactions into Quicken
Quicken for Windows CSV Import Apple Card transactions into Quicken for Windows
Actual Budget OFX Actual Budget
GnuCash OFX or QIF GnuCash
Excel or Numbers CSV Excel, Numbers

Wallet can also make an export file for a statement or a period. Make an export file of your Apple Card transactions gives the steps.

The summary in Wallet

Wallet shows a summary of your Apple Card transactions for a week, a month or a year. Apple's page See your Apple Card spending history gives the steps.

If your app is not in these lists

  1. In the help pages of your app, find "Apple Card".
  2. If the app cannot connect, find the file formats that the app can import.
  3. If the app can import a CSV, OFX or QIF file, add that file as a destination in WalletFeed.
