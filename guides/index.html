Guides

Each guide shows how to send Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to one app or file type with WalletFeed.

Budgeting apps

  • Lunch MoneySend Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Lunch Money with WalletFeed. You paste a Lunch Money access token into WalletFeed.
  • PocketSmithGet your Apple Card transactions into PocketSmith from your iPhone, with no file to import. Make a PocketSmith developer key, then paste it in WalletFeed.
  • Firefly IIISend Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Firefly III with WalletFeed. Make a personal access token, then paste it and your server address.
  • SureSend Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Sure, the self-hosted budget app that is a fork of Maybe Finance. Make an API key in Sure, then paste it.
  • Toshl FinanceSend your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Toshl Finance. Make a personal token in Toshl, then paste it into WalletFeed.
  • BankSyncSend Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a BankSync table. Then your AI agents can read them through BankSync's MCP tools or REST API.

Sheets and databases

  • Google SheetsSend your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Google Sheets spreadsheet automatically with WalletFeed.
  • AirtableSend your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to an Airtable base. WalletFeed adds each transaction as a record.
  • NotionSend your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Notion database with WalletFeed, for a budget in Notion.
  • CodaSend your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a table in Coda (Superhuman Docs) with WalletFeed and an API token.
  • SupabaseSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a Postgres database in Supabase with WalletFeed. Each transaction is a row in your project.
  • LinearSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Linear with WalletFeed. Each month, or each transaction, becomes an issue in a Linear team.
  • ExcelWalletFeed exports your Apple Card transactions to a CSV file on your iPhone. Excel opens the file on a Mac or a PC.
  • NumbersOpen your Apple Card transactions in a Numbers spreadsheet. WalletFeed saves them from your iPhone to a CSV file in iCloud Drive and updates the file.

Files and desktop apps

  • CSV fileExport your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a CSV file. WalletFeed writes the file in a folder that you select and updates it automatically.
  • OFX fileWalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions in one OFX file for each month. It updates the files automatically, and you import them into your app.
  • QIF fileExport your Apple Card transactions as QIF files. WalletFeed saves one QIF file for each month from your iPhone to a folder that you select.
  • QuickenYou can import your Apple Card transactions into Quicken for Mac from OFX files that WalletFeed saves in iCloud Drive. Quicken for Windows uses a CSV file.
  • Actual BudgetImport Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget. WalletFeed saves them from your iPhone to an OFX file each month, and you import the file on a computer.
  • GnuCashYou can import your Apple Card transactions into GnuCash. WalletFeed writes an OFX file for each month from your iPhone, and you import the files.
  • YNABImport your Apple Card transactions into YNAB with the YNAB app for iPhone, or with an OFX or CSV file that WalletFeed saves on your iPhone.

Automations

  • ZapierSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Zapier with WalletFeed. A Catch Hook trigger starts your Zap each month or for each transaction.
  • MakeSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a scenario in Make, previously Integromat. A Custom webhook receives each month or each transaction.
  • WebhookSend Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a webhook in n8n or on your server with WalletFeed.
  • ShortcutsMake a Shortcuts automation that sends each Apple Card purchase from your iPhone to your WalletFeed destinations in seconds.

Developers

  • AWSSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Amazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB or EventBridge in your AWS account with WalletFeed.

Free features

  • Spending by categorySee your Apple Card spending by category on your iPhone, with the total for the month and the merchants. You can use it without WalletFeed Pro.
  • SubscriptionsSee the subscriptions and other recurring charges on your Apple Card in WalletFeed. Get a notification when a new one starts or a price changes.
  • Search transactionsFind a purchase in your Apple Card transactions on your iPhone with Search in WalletFeed. Type a merchant, amount or category, or use Apple Intelligence.
  • Spending widgetAdd an Apple Card spending widget to the Home Screen or Lock Screen of your iPhone. It compares this month with the same day and time last month.

Export

  • Export transactionsExport or download your Apple Card transactions from Wallet, card.apple.com or WalletFeed. With WalletFeed Pro, WalletFeed can also update a file.
  • Apple Card APIThere is no web API for your Apple Card transactions. iPhone apps read them with FinanceKit. WalletFeed sends them to a webhook, AWS, Supabase or BankSync.
