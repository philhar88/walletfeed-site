Guides

Each guide shows how to send Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to one app or file type with WalletFeed.

Budgeting apps

  • Lunch MoneyWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Lunch Money. You paste a Lunch Money access token in WalletFeed.
  • PocketSmithWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions to PocketSmith with no import file. You make a developer key in PocketSmith and paste it in WalletFeed.
  • Firefly IIISend Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Firefly III. Make a personal access token in Firefly III. Then paste the token in WalletFeed.
  • SureSend Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Sure, a self-hosted fork of Maybe Finance. Make an API key in Sure. Then paste the key in WalletFeed.
  • Toshl FinanceSend your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Toshl Finance. Make a personal token in Toshl, then paste it into WalletFeed.
  • BankSyncWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a BankSync table. Your AI agents can read them with BankSync's MCP tools or REST API.

Sheets and databases

  • Google SheetsWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Google Sheets spreadsheet in your Google Drive.
  • AirtableWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to an Airtable base. Each transaction is a record.
  • NotionWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Notion database. You can use the database for a budget.
  • CodaWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a table in Coda (Superhuman Docs) with an API token.
  • SupabaseWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a Postgres database in Supabase. Each transaction is one row in a table.
  • LinearWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Linear. Each transaction becomes an issue in a Linear team.
  • ExcelWalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions to a CSV file on your iPhone. Excel opens the file on a Mac or a PC.
  • NumbersOpen your Apple Card transactions in a Numbers spreadsheet. WalletFeed saves them from your iPhone to a CSV file in iCloud Drive and updates the file.

Files and desktop apps

  • CSV fileWalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a CSV file in a folder that you select, and updates the file automatically.
  • OFX fileWalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions in one OFX file for each month and updates the files automatically. You import the files into your app.
  • QIF fileWalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone as QIF files. It writes one QIF file for each month to a folder that you select.
  • QuickenYou can import your Apple Card transactions into Quicken for Mac from OFX files that WalletFeed saves in iCloud Drive. Quicken for Windows uses a CSV file.
  • Actual BudgetYou can import Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget. WalletFeed writes them to an OFX file each month, and you import the file on a computer.
  • GnuCashYou can import your Apple Card transactions into GnuCash. On your iPhone, WalletFeed writes an OFX file for each month. Then, you import the files.
  • YNABYou can put your Apple Card transactions in YNAB with the YNAB app for iPhone. WalletFeed can also save them in OFX or CSV files that YNAB imports.

Automations

  • ZapierSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Zapier with WalletFeed. A Catch Hook trigger starts your Zap for each transaction and each change.
  • MakeSend your Apple Card transactions to Make (the previous name of Make is Integromat). A Custom webhook receives each new or changed transaction.
  • WebhookWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a webhook in n8n or on your server.
  • ShortcutsMake a Shortcuts automation that sends each Apple Card purchase to your WalletFeed destinations seconds after you tap your iPhone.

Developers

  • AWSSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Amazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB or EventBridge in your AWS account with WalletFeed.

Free features

  • Spending by categorySee your Apple Card spending by category on your iPhone, with the total for the month and the merchants. You can use Spending without WalletFeed Pro.
  • SubscriptionsFind the subscriptions and other recurring charges on your Apple Card in WalletFeed. Get a notification for a new recurring charge or a new price.
  • Search transactionsSearch in WalletFeed finds a purchase in your Apple Card transactions. You can type a merchant, an amount or a category, or use Apple Intelligence.
  • Spending widgetAdd an Apple Card spending widget to your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows this month and the month before this month, to the same day and time.

Export

  • Export filesYou can get an export file of your Apple Card transactions from Wallet, card.apple.com or WalletFeed. With WalletFeed Pro, WalletFeed also updates a file.
  • Apple Card APIThere is no web API for your Apple Card transactions. iPhone apps read them with FinanceKit. WalletFeed sends them to a webhook, AWS, Supabase or BankSync.
