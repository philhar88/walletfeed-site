Guides

Each guide shows how to send Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to one app or file type with WalletFeed.

Budgeting apps

  • Budget apps for Apple CardThe budgeting apps that support Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings on iPhone, from their help pages, and how WalletFeed sends transactions to other apps.
  • Lunch MoneyWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Lunch Money. You paste a Lunch Money access token in WalletFeed.
  • PocketSmithWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions to PocketSmith with no import file. You make a developer key in PocketSmith and paste it in WalletFeed.
  • Firefly IIISend Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Firefly III. Make a personal access token in Firefly III. Then paste the token in WalletFeed.
  • SureSend Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Sure, a self-hosted fork of Maybe Finance. Add a card account in Sure. Then use Sign In with Sure.
  • Toshl FinanceSend your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Toshl Finance. Make a personal token in Toshl, then paste it into WalletFeed.
  • Wallet by BudgetBakersWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions to Wallet by BudgetBakers with no import file. You paste an API token from BudgetBakers in WalletFeed.
  • BankSyncWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a BankSync table. Your AI agents can read them with BankSync's MCP tools or REST API.
  • Apps that use PlaidPlaid connects to Apple Card only in iPhone apps that added it, such as Rocket Money. This page gives the steps, the apps, and a procedure for other apps.
  • YNABWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions to YNAB with no import file. You make a personal access token in YNAB and paste it in WalletFeed.
  • QuickBooks OnlineWalletFeed sends your posted Apple Card transactions to a credit card account in QuickBooks Online, with no upload. You connect your company in WalletFeed.
  • XeroWalletFeed sends your posted Apple Card transactions to a bank account in Xero, with no statement file. You connect your Xero organization in WalletFeed.
  • Rocket MoneyRocket Money can link to Apple Card in its iPhone app, on iOS 17.4 or newer. The steps to link it, and what to look at if Apple Card does not connect.
  • MonarchMonarch can connect to Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings in its iPhone app. The steps, the limits, and what to do if Apple Card does not sync in Monarch.
  • Copilot MoneyYou can add Apple Card to Copilot Money only in its iPhone app, on iOS 17.4 or newer. The limits, and what to do if Apple Card does not sync with Copilot.

Sheets and databases

  • Google SheetsWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Google Sheets spreadsheet in your Google Drive.
  • Google Sheets templateA Google Sheets template with Spending and Budget tabs for your Apple Card transactions. You copy the tabs into the spreadsheet that WalletFeed makes.
  • AirtableWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to an Airtable base. Each transaction is a record.
  • NotionWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Notion database, with Sign In with Notion or a token.
  • Notion templateA Notion template with a WalletFeed Transactions database and views of your Apple Card spending by month and by category. WalletFeed sends the rows.
  • CodaWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a table in Coda (Superhuman Docs) with an API token.
  • SupabaseWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a Postgres database in Supabase. Each transaction is one row in a table.
  • LinearWalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Linear, as one issue for each month or one issue for each transaction.
  • ExcelWalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions to a CSV file on your iPhone. Excel opens the file on a Mac or a PC.
  • NumbersOpen your Apple Card transactions in a Numbers spreadsheet. WalletFeed saves them from your iPhone to a CSV file in iCloud Drive and updates the file.

AI chatbots

  • Ask an AI chatbotAsk ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok about your Apple Card spending. WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and the chatbot reads it from Google Drive.
  • ChatGPTTo connect your Apple Card to ChatGPT, WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and ChatGPT reads it. The page also gives the facts about Finances.
  • ClaudeAsk Claude about your Apple Card transactions. WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and Claude reads it with its Google Drive connector.
  • GeminiAsk Gemini about your Apple Card transactions. WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and Gemini reads it with the Google Workspace app.
  • GrokAsk Grok about your Apple Card transactions. WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and Grok reads it with its Google Drive connector.

Files and desktop apps

  • CSV fileWalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a CSV file in a folder that you select, and updates the file automatically.
  • OFX fileWalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions in one OFX file for each month and updates the files automatically. You import the files into your app.
  • QIF fileWalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone as QIF files. It writes one QIF file for each month to a folder that you select.
  • QuickenQuicken Classic has no connection to Apple Card. Import a QFX file from Wallet, or use the OFX files that WalletFeed saves for Quicken for Mac.
  • Quicken for WindowsQuicken Classic for Windows has no connection to Apple Card. Import a QFX file from Wallet, or the CSV file that WalletFeed saves for each month.
  • Actual BudgetYou can import Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget. WalletFeed writes them to an OFX file each month, and you import the file on a computer.
  • GnuCashYou can import your Apple Card transactions into GnuCash. On your iPhone, WalletFeed writes an OFX file for each month. Then, you import the files.

Automations

  • ZapierSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Zapier with WalletFeed. A Catch Hook trigger starts your Zap one time a month or for each change.
  • MakeSend your Apple Card transactions to Make (the previous name of Make is Integromat). A Custom webhook receives each month or each change.
  • WebhookWalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a webhook in n8n or on your server.
  • ShortcutsMake an Apple Pay automation in Shortcuts. The Transaction trigger starts when you tap your Apple Card, and WalletFeed sends the purchase.

Developers

  • AWSSend your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Amazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB or EventBridge in your AWS account with WalletFeed.
  • GitHubWalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions to a private GitHub repository, in a Beancount, hledger or CSV file for each month or each day.

Free features

  • Spending by categorySee your Apple Card spending by category: the spending summary in Wallet for a week, a month or a year, and the categories and merchants in WalletFeed.
  • SubscriptionsFind each subscription and recurring payment on your Apple Card in WalletFeed, with the cost for one month. Then cancel it on the correct Apple screen.
  • Search transactionsSearch in WalletFeed finds a purchase in your Apple Card transactions. You can type a merchant, an amount or a category, or use Apple Intelligence.
  • Spending widgetAdd an Apple Card spending widget to your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows this month, last month to the same day, and your card balance.
  • TaxesGet the year-end totals of your Apple Card and Savings transactions for your taxes: interest, fees, purchases and refunds, and a file of all transactions.
  • Year-end summaryFor an Apple Card year-end summary, Wallet shows your spending for a year. WalletFeed gives the annual totals and one file of the transactions of the year.

Export

  • Apple CardDownload your Apple Card transactions as a CSV, OFX, QFX or QBO file, or a PDF statement to print, in Wallet, at card.apple.com or in WalletFeed.
  • Apple Card APIThere is no web API for your Apple Card transactions. iPhone apps read them with FinanceKit. WalletFeed sends them to a webhook, AWS, Supabase or BankSync.
  • Apple CashWallet gives you a PDF file of your Apple Cash transactions in an e-mail. Export CSV in WalletFeed makes a CSV file of them on your iPhone.
  • SavingsDownload your Savings statements or an export file of your transactions in Wallet. Export CSV in WalletFeed makes a CSV file of a period that you select.
