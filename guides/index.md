# Guides

Each guide shows how to send Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to one app or file type with WalletFeed.

- [Ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok about your Apple Card spending](https://walletfeed.app/guides/ask-ai-about-apple-card-spending/index.md): Ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok about your Apple Card spending. WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and the chatbot reads it from Google Drive.
- [Connect your Apple Card to ChatGPT with a Google Sheet](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-chatgpt/index.md): To connect your Apple Card to ChatGPT, WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and ChatGPT reads it. The page also gives the facts about Finances.
- [How to ask Claude about your Apple Card transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-claude/index.md): Ask Claude about your Apple Card transactions. WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and Claude reads it with its Google Drive connector.
- [How to ask Gemini about your Apple Card transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-gemini/index.md): Ask Gemini about your Apple Card transactions. WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and Gemini reads it with the Google Workspace app.
- [How to ask Grok about your Apple Card transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-grok/index.md): Ask Grok about your Apple Card transactions. WalletFeed keeps a Google Sheet up to date, and Grok reads it with its Google Drive connector.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Zapier](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-zapier/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Zapier with WalletFeed. A Catch Hook trigger starts your Zap one time a month or for each change.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Make](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-make/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions to Make (the previous name of Make is Integromat). A Custom webhook receives each month or each change.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to a webhook](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-webhook/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a webhook in n8n or on your server.
- [Apple Pay automation: run a shortcut after each Apple Card purchase](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-shortcuts/index.md): Make an Apple Pay automation in Shortcuts. The Transaction trigger starts when you tap your Apple Card, and WalletFeed sends the purchase.
- [Budgeting apps that support Apple Card and Apple Cash](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-budget-apps/index.md): The budgeting apps that support Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings on iPhone, from their help pages, and how WalletFeed sends transactions to other apps.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Lunch Money](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-lunch-money/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Lunch Money. You paste a Lunch Money access token in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to PocketSmith](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-pocketsmith/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions to PocketSmith with no import file. You make a developer key in PocketSmith and paste it in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Firefly III](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-firefly-iii/index.md): Send Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Firefly III. Make a personal access token in Firefly III. Then paste the token in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Sure](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-sure/index.md): Send Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Sure, a self-hosted fork of Maybe Finance. Add a card account in Sure. Then use Sign In with Sure.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Toshl Finance](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-toshl/index.md): Send your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Toshl Finance. Make a personal token in Toshl, then paste it into WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Wallet by BudgetBakers](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-budgetbakers/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions to Wallet by BudgetBakers with no import file. You paste an API token from BudgetBakers in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to BankSync](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-banksync/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a BankSync table. Your AI agents can read them with BankSync's MCP tools or REST API.
- [Does Plaid support Apple Card? How to connect, and the apps that connect](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-plaid/index.md): Plaid connects to Apple Card only in iPhone apps that added it, such as Rocket Money. This page gives the steps, the apps, and a procedure for other apps.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to YNAB](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-ynab/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions to YNAB with no import file. You make a personal access token in YNAB and paste it in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to QuickBooks Online](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-quickbooks/index.md): WalletFeed sends your posted Apple Card transactions to a credit card account in QuickBooks Online, with no upload. You connect your company in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Xero](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-xero/index.md): WalletFeed sends your posted Apple Card transactions to a bank account in Xero, with no statement file. You connect your Xero organization in WalletFeed.
- [Link Apple Card to Rocket Money](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-rocket-money/index.md): Rocket Money can link to Apple Card in its iPhone app, on iOS 17.4 or newer. The steps to link it, and what to look at if Apple Card does not connect.
- [Connect Apple Card to Monarch](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-monarch/index.md): Monarch can connect to Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings in its iPhone app. The steps, the limits, and what to do if Apple Card does not sync in Monarch.
- [Add Apple Card to Copilot Money](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-copilot/index.md): You can add Apple Card to Copilot Money only in its iPhone app, on iOS 17.4 or newer. The limits, and what to do if Apple Card does not sync with Copilot.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to AWS](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-aws/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Amazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB or EventBridge in your AWS account with WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to GitHub](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-github/index.md): WalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions to a private GitHub repository, in a Beancount, hledger or CSV file for each month or each day.
- [Download your Apple Card transactions and statements](https://walletfeed.app/guides/export-apple-card-transactions/index.md): Download your Apple Card transactions as a CSV, OFX, QFX or QBO file, or a PDF statement to print, in Wallet, at card.apple.com or in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to your app or server](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-api/index.md): There is no web API for your Apple Card transactions. iPhone apps read them with FinanceKit. WalletFeed sends them to a webhook, AWS, Supabase or BankSync.
- [Make a CSV export file of your Apple Cash transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/export-apple-cash-transactions/index.md): Wallet gives you a PDF file of your Apple Cash transactions in an e-mail. Export CSV in WalletFeed makes a CSV file of them on your iPhone.
- [Make an export file of your Savings transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/export-apple-savings-transactions/index.md): Download your Savings statements or an export file of your transactions in Wallet. Export CSV in WalletFeed makes a CSV file of a period that you select.
- [Save Apple Card transactions to a CSV file automatically](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-csv/index.md): WalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a CSV file in a folder that you select, and updates the file automatically.
- [Save Apple Card transactions as OFX files](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-ofx/index.md): WalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions in one OFX file for each month and updates the files automatically. You import the files into your app.
- [Save Apple Card transactions as QIF files](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-qif/index.md): WalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone as QIF files. It writes one QIF file for each month to a folder that you select.
- [Add Apple Card transactions to Quicken](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-quicken/index.md): Quicken Classic has no connection to Apple Card. Import a QFX file from Wallet, or use the OFX files that WalletFeed saves for Quicken for Mac.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into Quicken for Windows](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-quicken-for-windows/index.md): Quicken Classic for Windows has no connection to Apple Card. Import a QFX file from Wallet, or the CSV file that WalletFeed saves for each month.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-actual-budget/index.md): You can import Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget. WalletFeed writes them to an OFX file each month, and you import the file on a computer.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into GnuCash](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-gnucash/index.md): You can import your Apple Card transactions into GnuCash. On your iPhone, WalletFeed writes an OFX file for each month. Then, you import the files.
- [See Apple Card spending by category](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-spending-by-category/index.md): See your Apple Card spending by category: the spending summary in Wallet for a week, a month or a year, and the categories and merchants in WalletFeed.
- [Find and cancel subscriptions on your Apple Card](https://walletfeed.app/guides/find-apple-card-subscriptions/index.md): Find each subscription and recurring payment on your Apple Card in WalletFeed, with the cost for one month. Then cancel it on the correct Apple screen.
- [Search Apple Card transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/search-apple-card-transactions/index.md): Search in WalletFeed finds a purchase in your Apple Card transactions. You can type a merchant, an amount or a category, or use Apple Intelligence.
- [Add an Apple Card spending widget](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-spending-widget/index.md): Add an Apple Card spending widget to your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows this month, last month to the same day, and your card balance.
- [Get your Apple Card totals and transactions for your taxes](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-taxes/index.md): Get the year-end totals of your Apple Card and Savings transactions for your taxes: interest, fees, purchases and refunds, and a file of all transactions.
- [Get an Apple Card year-end summary](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-year-end-summary/index.md): For an Apple Card year-end summary, Wallet shows your spending for a year. WalletFeed gives the annual totals and one file of the transactions of the year.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Google Sheets](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-google-sheets/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Google Sheets spreadsheet in your Google Drive.
- [Apple Card budget template for Google Sheets](https://walletfeed.app/guides/google-sheets-budget-template/index.md): A Google Sheets template with Spending and Budget tabs for your Apple Card transactions. You copy the tabs into the spreadsheet that WalletFeed makes.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Airtable](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-airtable/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to an Airtable base. Each transaction is a record.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Notion](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-notion/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Notion database, with Sign In with Notion or a token.
- [Apple Card budget template for Notion](https://walletfeed.app/guides/notion-budget-template/index.md): A Notion template with a WalletFeed Transactions database and views of your Apple Card spending by month and by category. WalletFeed sends the rows.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Coda](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-coda/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a table in Coda (Superhuman Docs) with an API token.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Supabase](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-supabase/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a Postgres database in Supabase. Each transaction is one row in a table.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Linear](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-linear/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Linear, as one issue for each month or one issue for each transaction.
- [Open Apple Card transactions in Excel](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-excel/index.md): WalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions to a CSV file on your iPhone. Excel opens the file on a Mac or a PC.
- [Open Apple Card transactions in Numbers](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-numbers/index.md): Open your Apple Card transactions in a Numbers spreadsheet. WalletFeed saves them from your iPhone to a CSV file in iCloud Drive and updates the file.
