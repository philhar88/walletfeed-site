# Guides

Each guide shows how to send Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to one app or file type with WalletFeed.

- [Send Apple Card transactions to Zapier](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-zapier/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Zapier with WalletFeed. A Catch Hook trigger starts your Zap each month or for each transaction.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Make](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-make/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a scenario in Make, previously Integromat. A Custom webhook receives each month or each transaction.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to a webhook](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-webhook/index.md): Send Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a webhook in n8n or on your server with WalletFeed.
- [Run a shortcut after each Apple Card purchase](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-shortcuts/index.md): Make a Shortcuts automation that sends each Apple Card purchase from your iPhone to your WalletFeed destinations in seconds.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Lunch Money](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-lunch-money/index.md): Send Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Lunch Money with WalletFeed. You paste a Lunch Money access token into WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to PocketSmith](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-pocketsmith/index.md): Get your Apple Card transactions into PocketSmith from your iPhone, with no file to import. Make a PocketSmith developer key, then paste it in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Firefly III](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-firefly-iii/index.md): Send Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Firefly III with WalletFeed. Make a personal access token, then paste it and your server address.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Sure](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-sure/index.md): Send Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Sure, the self-hosted budget app that is a fork of Maybe Finance. Make an API key in Sure, then paste it.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Toshl Finance](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-toshl/index.md): Send your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Toshl Finance. Make a personal token in Toshl, then paste it into WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to BankSync](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-banksync/index.md): Send Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a BankSync table. Then your AI agents can read them through BankSync's MCP tools or REST API.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to AWS](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-aws/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Amazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB or EventBridge in your AWS account with WalletFeed.
- [Export Apple Card transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/export-apple-card-transactions/index.md): Export or download your Apple Card transactions from Wallet, card.apple.com or WalletFeed. With WalletFeed Pro, WalletFeed can also update a file.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to your app or server](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-api/index.md): There is no web API for your Apple Card transactions. iPhone apps read them with FinanceKit. WalletFeed sends them to a webhook, AWS, Supabase or BankSync.
- [Export Apple Card transactions to a CSV file automatically](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-csv/index.md): Export your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a CSV file. WalletFeed writes the file in a folder that you select and updates it automatically.
- [Save Apple Card transactions as OFX files](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-ofx/index.md): WalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions in one OFX file for each month. It updates the files automatically, and you import them into your app.
- [Save Apple Card transactions as QIF files](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-qif/index.md): Export your Apple Card transactions as QIF files. WalletFeed saves one QIF file for each month from your iPhone to a folder that you select.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into Quicken](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-quicken/index.md): You can import your Apple Card transactions into Quicken for Mac from OFX files that WalletFeed saves in iCloud Drive. Quicken for Windows uses a CSV file.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-actual-budget/index.md): Import Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget. WalletFeed saves them from your iPhone to an OFX file each month, and you import the file on a computer.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into GnuCash](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-gnucash/index.md): You can import your Apple Card transactions into GnuCash. WalletFeed writes an OFX file for each month from your iPhone, and you import the files.
- [Get Apple Card transactions into YNAB](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-ynab/index.md): Import your Apple Card transactions into YNAB with the YNAB app for iPhone, or with an OFX or CSV file that WalletFeed saves on your iPhone.
- [See Apple Card spending by category](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-spending-by-category/index.md): See your Apple Card spending by category on your iPhone, with the total for the month and the merchants. You can use it without WalletFeed Pro.
- [Find subscriptions on your Apple Card](https://walletfeed.app/guides/find-apple-card-subscriptions/index.md): See the subscriptions and other recurring charges on your Apple Card in WalletFeed. Get a notification when a new one starts or a price changes.
- [Search Apple Card transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/search-apple-card-transactions/index.md): Find a purchase in your Apple Card transactions on your iPhone with Search in WalletFeed. Type a merchant, amount or category, or use Apple Intelligence.
- [Add an Apple Card spending widget](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-spending-widget/index.md): Add an Apple Card spending widget to the Home Screen or Lock Screen of your iPhone. It compares this month with the same day and time last month.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Google Sheets](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-google-sheets/index.md): Send your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Google Sheets spreadsheet automatically with WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Airtable](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-airtable/index.md): Send your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to an Airtable base. WalletFeed adds each transaction as a record.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Notion](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-notion/index.md): Send your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Notion database with WalletFeed, for a budget in Notion.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Coda](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-coda/index.md): Send your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a table in Coda (Superhuman Docs) with WalletFeed and an API token.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Supabase](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-supabase/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a Postgres database in Supabase with WalletFeed. Each transaction is a row in your project.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Linear](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-linear/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Linear with WalletFeed. Each month, or each transaction, becomes an issue in a Linear team.
- [Open Apple Card transactions in Excel](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-excel/index.md): WalletFeed exports your Apple Card transactions to a CSV file on your iPhone. Excel opens the file on a Mac or a PC.
- [Open Apple Card transactions in Numbers](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-numbers/index.md): Open your Apple Card transactions in a Numbers spreadsheet. WalletFeed saves them from your iPhone to a CSV file in iCloud Drive and updates the file.
