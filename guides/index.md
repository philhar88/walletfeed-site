# Guides

Each guide shows how to send Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to one app or file type with WalletFeed.

- [Send Apple Card transactions to Zapier](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-zapier/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Zapier with WalletFeed. A Catch Hook trigger starts your Zap for each transaction and each change.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Make](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-make/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions to Make (the previous name of Make is Integromat). A Custom webhook receives each new or changed transaction.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to a webhook](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-webhook/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a webhook in n8n or on your server.
- [Run a shortcut after each Apple Card purchase](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-shortcuts/index.md): Make a Shortcuts automation that sends each Apple Card purchase to your WalletFeed destinations seconds after you tap your iPhone.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Lunch Money](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-lunch-money/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Lunch Money. You paste a Lunch Money access token in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to PocketSmith](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-pocketsmith/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions to PocketSmith with no import file. You make a developer key in PocketSmith and paste it in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Firefly III](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-firefly-iii/index.md): Send Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Firefly III. Make a personal access token in Firefly III. Then paste the token in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Sure](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-sure/index.md): Send Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Sure, a self-hosted fork of Maybe Finance. Make an API key in Sure. Then paste the key in WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Toshl Finance](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-toshl/index.md): Send your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to Toshl Finance. Make a personal token in Toshl, then paste it into WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to BankSync](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-banksync/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a BankSync table. Your AI agents can read them with BankSync's MCP tools or REST API.
- [Put Apple Card transactions in an app that uses Plaid](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-plaid/index.md): You can connect your Apple Card to Plaid only in an iPhone app that reads Wallet. For a different app, use an export file from Wallet or WalletFeed.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to AWS](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-aws/index.md): Send your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Amazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB or EventBridge in your AWS account with WalletFeed.
- [Make an export file of your Apple Card transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/export-apple-card-transactions/index.md): You can get an export file of your Apple Card transactions from Wallet, card.apple.com or WalletFeed. With WalletFeed Pro, WalletFeed also updates a file.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to your app or server](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-api/index.md): There is no web API for your Apple Card transactions. iPhone apps read them with FinanceKit. WalletFeed sends them to a webhook, AWS, Supabase or BankSync.
- [Save Apple Card transactions to a CSV file automatically](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-csv/index.md): WalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a CSV file in a folder that you select, and updates the file automatically.
- [Save Apple Card transactions as OFX files](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-ofx/index.md): WalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions in one OFX file for each month and updates the files automatically. You import the files into your app.
- [Save Apple Card transactions as QIF files](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-qif/index.md): WalletFeed saves your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone as QIF files. It writes one QIF file for each month to a folder that you select.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into Quicken](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-quicken/index.md): You can import your Apple Card transactions into Quicken for Mac from OFX files that WalletFeed saves in iCloud Drive. Quicken for Windows uses a CSV file.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-actual-budget/index.md): You can import Apple Card transactions into Actual Budget. WalletFeed writes them to an OFX file each month, and you import the file on a computer.
- [Import Apple Card transactions into GnuCash](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-gnucash/index.md): You can import your Apple Card transactions into GnuCash. On your iPhone, WalletFeed writes an OFX file for each month. Then, you import the files.
- [Put Apple Card transactions in YNAB](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-ynab/index.md): You can put your Apple Card transactions in YNAB with the YNAB app for iPhone. WalletFeed can also save them in OFX or CSV files that YNAB imports.
- [Upload Apple Card transactions to QuickBooks Online](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-quickbooks/index.md): Put your Apple Card transactions in QuickBooks Online with a file. WalletFeed saves them in OFX or CSV files, and you upload the files in QuickBooks.
- [See Apple Card spending by category](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-spending-by-category/index.md): See your Apple Card spending by category on your iPhone, with the total for the month and the merchants. You can use Spending without WalletFeed Pro.
- [Find subscriptions on your Apple Card](https://walletfeed.app/guides/find-apple-card-subscriptions/index.md): Find the subscriptions and other recurring charges on your Apple Card in WalletFeed. Get a notification for a new recurring charge or a new price.
- [Search Apple Card transactions](https://walletfeed.app/guides/search-apple-card-transactions/index.md): Search in WalletFeed finds a purchase in your Apple Card transactions. You can type a merchant, an amount or a category, or use Apple Intelligence.
- [Add an Apple Card spending widget](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-spending-widget/index.md): Add an Apple Card spending widget to your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows this month and the month before this month, to the same day and time.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Google Sheets](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-google-sheets/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Google Sheets spreadsheet in your Google Drive.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Airtable](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-airtable/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to an Airtable base. Each transaction is a record.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Notion](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-notion/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a Notion database. You can use the database for a budget.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Coda](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-coda/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions from your iPhone to a table in Coda (Superhuman Docs) with an API token.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Supabase](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-supabase/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to a Postgres database in Supabase. Each transaction is one row in a table.
- [Send Apple Card transactions to Linear](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-linear/index.md): WalletFeed sends your Apple Card transactions from your iPhone to Linear. Each transaction becomes an issue in a Linear team.
- [Open Apple Card transactions in Excel](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-excel/index.md): WalletFeed writes your Apple Card transactions to a CSV file on your iPhone. Excel opens the file on a Mac or a PC.
- [Open Apple Card transactions in Numbers](https://walletfeed.app/guides/apple-card-to-numbers/index.md): Open your Apple Card transactions in a Numbers spreadsheet. WalletFeed saves them from your iPhone to a CSV file in iCloud Drive and updates the file.
