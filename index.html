Spending for Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings

WalletFeed reads your transactions from Wallet and shows your spending on your iPhone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, it also summarizes your month and interprets searches. It never stores your transactions.

Coming soon to the App Store

Spending

  • TransactionsTransactions from the Wallet accounts you share, grouped by month.
  • SpendingThis month by day, category and merchant, compared with the same point last month.
  • Recurring chargesCharges that repeat, such as subscriptions, found in your history.
  • Search and exportSearch all your transactions, and export a CSV file when you need one.
  • SiriAsk Siri to open Spending or start a search.

Apple Intelligence

  • Monthly summaryTwo sentences about your month, written from figures WalletFeed calculates.
  • SearchType “dining over $50 last month” to see those transactions and their total.
  • Recurring charge names“NETFLIX.COM 866-579” appears as Netflix, with the kind of service.

The model runs on your iPhone and sees only what each feature needs. Requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence.

WalletFeed Pro

WalletFeed Pro sends new and changed transactions from your iPhone to the destinations you add.

  • Budgeting appsLunch Money, PocketSmith, Firefly III, Sure, Toshl Finance and BankSync
  • Sheets and databasesGoogle Sheets, Airtable, Notion, Coda, Supabase and Linear
  • AutomationsZapier, Make and signed webhooks
  • FilesOne CSV, OFX or QIF file a month in iCloud Drive or another folder
  • AWSAmazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB and EventBridge

Privacy

  • No accountThere’s nothing to sign up for, and WalletFeed has no servers.
  • On your iPhoneApple Intelligence features run on your iPhone, and destinations receive transactions directly from it.
  • Transactions aren’t keptWalletFeed reads transactions from Wallet when it needs them and doesn’t keep a copy. Keys you paste are saved in the Keychain.
