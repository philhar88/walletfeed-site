Understand your spending.

Walletfeed turns your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions into clear insights, with Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Walletfeed never stores your transactions.

Apple Intelligence, on your iPhone

  • Ask in your own words“Dining over $50 last month” finds the transactions and adds them up. Or ask Siri to search Walletfeed.
  • Your month, explainedWhat changed, in two sentences, with every figure calculated by Walletfeed.
  • Subscriptions, namedRecurring charges found, and “NETFLIX.COM 866-579” shown as Netflix, Streaming.

The model runs on your iPhone and sees only what each feature needs. Requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence.

Spending insights, free

  • TransactionsEvery purchase, refund and transfer, grouped by month.
  • SpendingBy day, category and merchant, compared with last month.
  • Search and exportYour whole history, and a CSV whenever you want one.

Sync with Walletfeed Pro

New and changed transactions go straight from your iPhone to the tools you already use. Pending charges update when they post; declined ones are removed.

  • BudgetingLunch Money, PocketSmith, Firefly III, Sure, Toshl and BankSync
  • Sheets and databasesGoogle Sheets, Airtable, Notion, Coda, Supabase and Linear
  • AutomationsZapier, Make and signed webhooks
  • FilesCSV, OFX or QIF, one file a month in iCloud Drive or any folder
  • DevelopersAmazon S3, SQS, Kinesis, DynamoDB and EventBridge

Private by design

  • On your iPhoneApple Intelligence runs on device. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • No account, no serversThere's nothing to sign up for, and nothing passes through Walletfeed.
  • Never storedTransactions are read from Wallet when needed. Keys stay in your Keychain.
