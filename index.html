Questions

  • How do new purchases reach my apps while WalletFeed is closed?After a change in Wallet, Apple’s FinanceKit starts WalletFeed in the background, and Background Sync updates your destinations within about an hour.
  • What happens when a charge posts, changes or is declined?WalletFeed matches each charge by its Wallet ID and updates it in place, so it doesn’t arrive twice. A declined charge is removed.
  • Does WalletFeed stay within each service’s limits?Yes. WalletFeed puts many transactions in one request where a service accepts that, and it spaces its requests to fit each service’s limits.
  • What’s the difference between Monthly and Each Transaction?Monthly sends one Linear issue, or one Zapier or Make event, a month. Each Transaction sends every change as it happens.
  • Which accounts can WalletFeed read?WalletFeed reads Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings, the Wallet accounts that Apple’s FinanceKit lets apps read in the US.
  • Is there a version for iPad, Mac, Android or the web?No. Apple makes Wallet transactions available to apps only on iPhone.
