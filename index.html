Spending for Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings

Ask about your spending in your own words, and Apple Intelligence on your iPhone answers with a chart and a summary. WalletFeed needs no account and has no servers of its own. Your transactions go only where you send them.

Coming soon to the App Store

Apple Intelligence

  • Ask SpendingAsk “dining this month vs last” to see a chart, the categories, the matching transactions and a summary.
  • Monthly SummaryA short summary of your month, written from figures WalletFeed calculates.
  • SearchType “dining over $50 last month” to see those transactions and their total.
  • Recurring charge names“NETFLIX.COM 866-579” appears as Netflix, with the kind of service.

WalletFeed calculates every chart, total and list. Apple Intelligence runs on your iPhone and sees only what each feature needs. Requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Privacy

  • No accountThere’s nothing to sign up for, and WalletFeed doesn’t know who you are.
  • No trackingWalletFeed has no analytics, advertising or third-party code.
  • No WalletFeed serversWalletFeed reads transactions from Wallet on your iPhone when it needs them and doesn’t keep a copy.
  • On your iPhoneApple Intelligence features run on the device, and nothing is sent to a server.
  • Destinations you addWith Pro, transactions go straight from your iPhone to the services you choose. Keys and sign-ins stay in the Keychain on your iPhone.

Spending

  • SpendingThis month by day, category and merchant, compared with the same point last month.
  • TransactionsTransactions from the Wallet accounts you share, grouped by month.
  • Recurring ChargesSubscriptions and other charges that repeat, found in your history.
  • Recurring Charge AlertsA notification when a charge starts repeating or its price changes.
  • Monthly InstallmentsApple Card Monthly Installments charges, in their own section of Spending.
  • Spending widgetThis month’s spending and the same point last month, on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
  • Search and exportSearch all your transactions, and export a CSV file when you need one.
  • Siri and ShortcutsAsk Siri to show your spending or search your transactions.

WalletFeed Pro

WalletFeed Pro is optional. It sends new and changed transactions from your iPhone to the destinations you add.

  • Budgeting appsBankSync, Lunch Money, PocketSmith, Firefly III, Sure and Toshl Finance. BankSync also offers Sign In with BankSync.
  • Sheets and databasesGoogle Sheets, Airtable, Notion, Coda, Supabase and Linear
  • AutomationsZapier, Make and signed webhooks
  • FilesOne CSV file, or a CSV, OFX or QIF file a month, in iCloud Drive or another folder
  • AWSAmazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB and EventBridge
  • Background SyncYour destinations update within the hour, even when WalletFeed is closed.
  • Send HistoryBy default, a new destination also receives your earlier transactions, at a pace the service accepts. Zapier, Make and Linear start 30 days back.
  • ShortcutsSync after each card tap, or get recent transactions in a shortcut.
