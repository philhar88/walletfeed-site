# WalletFeed

Spending for Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings

Ask about your spending in your own words, and Apple Intelligence on your iPhone answers with a chart and a summary. WalletFeed needs no account, and no WalletFeed server keeps your transactions. Your transactions go only where you send them.

Coming soon to the App Store

## Apple Intelligence

- Ask Spending: Ask “dining this month vs last” to see a chart, the categories, the matching transactions and a summary.
- Monthly Summary: A short summary of your month, written from figures WalletFeed calculates.
- Search: Type “dining over $50 last month” to see those transactions and their total.
- Recurring charge names: “NETFLIX.COM 866-579” appears as Netflix, with the kind of service.

WalletFeed calculates every chart, total and list. Apple Intelligence runs on your iPhone and sees only what each feature needs. Requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

## Privacy

- No account: There’s nothing to sign up for, and WalletFeed doesn’t know who you are.
- No tracking: WalletFeed has no analytics, advertising or third-party code.
- No copy on a WalletFeed server: WalletFeed reads transactions from Wallet on your iPhone when it needs them and doesn’t keep a copy.
- On your iPhone: Apple Intelligence features run on the device, and nothing is sent to a server.
- Destinations you add: With Pro, transactions go straight from your iPhone to the services you choose. Keys and sign-ins stay in the Keychain on your iPhone.

Read the [privacy policy](https://walletfeed.app/privacy/).

## Spending

- Spending: This month by day, category and merchant, compared with the same point last month.
- Transactions: Transactions from the Wallet accounts you share, grouped by month.
- Recurring Charges: Subscriptions and other charges that repeat, found in your history.
- Recurring Charge Alerts: A notification when a charge starts repeating or its price changes.
- Monthly Installments: Apple Card Monthly Installments charges, in their own section of Spending.
- Spending widget: This month’s spending and the same point last month, on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
- Search and export: Search all your transactions, and export a CSV file when you need one.
- Siri and Shortcuts: Ask Siri to show your spending or search your transactions.

## WalletFeed Pro

WalletFeed Pro is optional. It sends new and changed transactions from your iPhone to the destinations you add.

- Budgeting apps: BankSync, Lunch Money, PocketSmith, Firefly III, Sure, Toshl Finance, Wallet by BudgetBakers and YNAB. BankSync also offers Sign In with BankSync.
- Accounting: QuickBooks Online, as credit card expenses and credits
- Sheets and databases: Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, Coda, Supabase and Linear
- Automations: Zapier, Make and signed [webhooks](https://walletfeed.app/docs/webhooks/)
- Files: One CSV file, or a CSV, OFX, QIF or Quicken CSV file a month, in iCloud Drive or another folder
- Developers: AWS (Amazon S3, SQS, Kinesis Data Streams, DynamoDB and EventBridge), and GitHub, with a Beancount, hledger or CSV file a month in a private repository

- Background Sync: Your destinations update within the hour, even when WalletFeed is closed.
- Send History: By default, a new destination also receives your earlier transactions, at a pace the service accepts. With Send set to Each Transaction, Zapier, Make and Linear start 30 days back.
- Shortcuts: Sync after each card tap, or get recent transactions in a shortcut.

## Questions

- [How do new purchases reach my apps while WalletFeed is closed?](https://walletfeed.app/faq/#background-sync): After a change in Wallet, Apple’s FinanceKit starts WalletFeed in the background, and Background Sync updates your destinations within about an hour.
- [What happens when a charge posts, changes or is declined?](https://walletfeed.app/faq/#changes): WalletFeed matches each charge by its Wallet ID and updates it in place, so it doesn’t arrive twice. A declined charge is removed.
- [Does WalletFeed stay within each service’s limits?](https://walletfeed.app/faq/#limits): Yes. WalletFeed puts many transactions in one request where a service accepts that, and it spaces its requests to fit each service’s limits.
- [What’s the difference between Monthly and Each Transaction?](https://walletfeed.app/faq/#monthly): Monthly sends one Linear issue, or one Zapier or Make event, a month. Each Transaction sends every change as it happens.
- [Which accounts can WalletFeed read?](https://walletfeed.app/faq/#accounts): WalletFeed reads Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings, the Wallet accounts that Apple’s FinanceKit lets apps read in the US.
- [Is there a version for iPad, Mac, Android or the web?](https://walletfeed.app/faq/#other-devices): No. Apple makes Wallet transactions available to apps only on iPhone.

Read the [FAQ](https://walletfeed.app/faq/).
