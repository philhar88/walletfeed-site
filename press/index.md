# Press

WalletFeed is a spending app for Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings on iPhone.

Ask about your spending in your own words, such as “dining this month vs last”, and Apple Intelligence on your iPhone answers with a chart, the categories, the matching transactions and a summary. WalletFeed Pro can also send new purchases from your iPhone to your budgeting app or spreadsheet.

You don’t need an account, and no WalletFeed server keeps your transactions or keys. WalletFeed reads your transactions from Wallet on your iPhone when it needs them and doesn’t keep a copy.

[Available on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6827429675)

## Key facts

| Price | Free. The spending features, including the Apple Intelligence ones, are free. WalletFeed Pro costs $0.99 a month or $4.99 a year, each with a 1-week free trial, or $19.99 once for Lifetime. It works with Family Sharing. |
| --- | --- |
| Requires | iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The Apple Intelligence features need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. |
| Region | US |
| Accounts | Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings |
| Destinations | With WalletFeed Pro: YNAB, Lunch Money, PocketSmith, Firefly III, Sure, Toshl Finance, Wallet by BudgetBakers, BankSync, QuickBooks Online, Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, Coda, Supabase, Linear, Zapier, Make, signed webhooks, GitHub, AWS, and CSV, OFX, QIF or Quicken CSV files |
| Privacy | No account, analytics, advertising or third-party code. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. |
| Version 1.1 | YNAB, QuickBooks Online, Wallet by BudgetBakers, GitHub, Quicken CSV files, and layouts for iPhone Duo |
| Developer | Phillip Harrison |
