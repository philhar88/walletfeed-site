# Press kit: screenshots, icon and facts

Press kit

WalletFeed is a spending app for Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings on iPhone.

Ask about your spending in your own words, such as “dining this month vs last”, and Apple Intelligence on your iPhone answers with a chart, the categories, the matching transactions and a summary. WalletFeed Pro can also send new purchases from your iPhone to your budgeting app or spreadsheet.

You don’t need an account, and no WalletFeed server keeps your transactions or keys. WalletFeed reads your transactions from Wallet on your iPhone when it needs them and doesn’t keep a copy.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6827429675?pt=125538749&ct=press&mt=8)

| Price | Free. The spending features, including the Apple Intelligence ones, are free. WalletFeed Pro costs $0.99 a month or $4.99 a year, each with a 1-week free trial, or $19.99 once for Lifetime. It works with Family Sharing. |
| --- | --- |
| Requires | iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The Apple Intelligence features need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence. |
| Region | US |
| Accounts | Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings |
| Destinations | With WalletFeed Pro: YNAB, Lunch Money, PocketSmith, Firefly III, Sure, Toshl Finance, Wallet by BudgetBakers, BankSync, QuickBooks Online, Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, Coda, Supabase, Linear, Zapier, Make, signed webhooks, GitHub, AWS, and CSV, OFX, QIF or Quicken CSV files |
| Privacy | No account, analytics, advertising or third-party code. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. |
| Free features | Spending by day, category and merchant; Ask Spending, Monthly Summary and Search with Apple Intelligence; Accounts, with each balance over time; Subscriptions and Recurring Charges, with alerts; the Tax Pack’s totals; a Home Screen and Lock Screen widget; CSV export; Siri and Shortcuts |
| Latest version | 1.4.1, November 2026. Version 1.4 added the Tax Pack, Subscriptions and a screen for each account. |
| First released | November 2026 |
| Developer | Phillip Harrison |
| Contact | [support@walletfeed.app](mailto:support@walletfeed.app?subject=Press) |

## What’s new

- 1.4.1, November 2026: The WalletFeed Pro screen shows when a plan is on sale: the usual price and how much is off.
- 1.4, November 2026: The Tax Pack: a year’s interest, fees, purchases and refunds for each account, free to see, with files to share on Pro Yearly and Lifetime. Subscriptions: every charge that repeats, with its cost each month and each year. A screen for each account, with its balance over a week to a year.
- 1.3, November 2026: Sign In with GitHub, and GitHub files kept up to date hourly, daily or with each transaction. Sure opens on its own sign-in.
- 1.2, November 2026: Accounts on the Spending tab: the Apple Card balance, available credit and due date, with Apple Cash and Savings. Payment Due Reminder, Monthly Recap, and “Ask WalletFeed” with Siri. Destinations receive more of Wallet’s details.
- 1.1, November 2026: New destinations: YNAB, QuickBooks Online, Wallet by BudgetBakers, GitHub and Quicken CSV files. Layouts for iPhone Duo. Sign-in for Notion.
- 1.0, November 2026: The first version: spending for Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings with Apple Intelligence, and destinations with WalletFeed Pro.
