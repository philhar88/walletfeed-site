WalletFeed Pro lets WalletFeed send your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings transactions to [destinations](/docs/destinations/), for example a budget app or a spreadsheet. WalletFeed Pro is a subscription with a 1-week free trial, or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

You can use these functions without WalletFeed Pro:

- [Spending](/docs/spending/)
- [Ask Spending](/docs/ask-spending/)
- [Search](/docs/search/)
- [Recurring Charges](/docs/recurring-charges/)
- The [Spending widget](/docs/spending-widget/)
- [Export CSV](/docs/export-csv/).

## Functions of WalletFeed Pro

With WalletFeed Pro, you can add destinations, for example Lunch Money or Google Sheets. WalletFeed sends your transactions to each destination. While WalletFeed is open, a destination receives changes in seconds.

When WalletFeed is closed, [Background Sync](/docs/background-sync/) also sends changes to your destinations. iOS starts WalletFeed in approximately 1 hour or less after a change in Wallet.

A new destination also receives previous transactions, from the start date in **Settings** › **Sync** › **Initial History**. **Initial History** is **All** until you change it. If **Send** is **Each Transaction** for Zapier, Make or Linear, the destination receives a maximum of 30 days of previous transactions. On the destination screen, [Send History](/docs/send-history/) sends more of them.

WalletFeed Pro adds the **Sync** section to **Settings**.

## Plans and prices

The **WalletFeed Pro** screen shows these plans:

| Plan | Price | Free trial |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yearly** | $4.99 a year ($0.42 a month) | 1 week |
| **Monthly** | $0.99 a month | 1 week |
| **Lifetime** | $19.99, one time | No free trial |

**Yearly** and **Monthly** are subscriptions. **Lifetime** is a one-time purchase.

You can get one free trial of WalletFeed Pro, for **Yearly** or for **Monthly**. After the free trial, the subscription continues at the price of the plan. If you cannot get a free trial, the button at the bottom of the screen shows **Subscribe** for **Yearly** and **Monthly**.

You can use Family Sharing with WalletFeed Pro.

## Get WalletFeed Pro

1. On your iPhone, open WalletFeed.
2. Tap **Settings** (the gear button) at the top of the screen.

   ![The Transactions screen with the gear button at the top.](app:06-transactions)

3. In the **Subscription** section, tap the **WalletFeed Pro** row that shows **Upgrade**.

   **Settings** closes, and the **WalletFeed Pro** screen opens. It shows the plans and their prices. **Yearly** has the check mark.

   ![The WalletFeed Pro screen with a check mark on the Yearly plan, and the Start Free Trial button at the bottom.](app:10-pro)

4. Tap a plan.

   The text below the button at the bottom gives the terms, for example "1 week free, then $4.99/year."

5. Tap the button at the bottom of the screen, for example **Start Free Trial** or **Buy Lifetime**.
6. Complete the purchase on the App Store screen.

   The **WalletFeed Pro** screen closes. When you open **Settings** again, the **Subscription** section shows your plan, for example **Yearly**.

## Get WalletFeed Pro when you add a destination

Without WalletFeed Pro, you can see all the destinations in **Add Destination**. When you tap a destination, WalletFeed shows the **WalletFeed Pro** screen.

1. In WalletFeed, tap **Destinations**.
2. Tap **+** at the top of the screen.

   The **Add Destination** screen opens.

   ![The Add Destination screen with the Budgeting section and the Sheets & Databases section.](app:08-add-destination)

3. Tap a destination, for example PocketSmith.

   The **WalletFeed Pro** screen opens. It shows the icon of the destination above the title.

   ![The WalletFeed Pro screen with the PocketSmith icon above the title.](app:docs-store-destination)

4. Tap a plan.
5. Tap the button at the bottom of the screen, for example **Start Free Trial**.
6. Complete the purchase on the App Store screen.

   The **WalletFeed Pro** screen changes to the setup form of the destination.

If you have a plan, you can tap **Restore Purchases** below the plans as an alternative to steps 4 to 6. When WalletFeed finds your plan, the setup form opens.

## Get WalletFeed Pro on a different iPhone

When WalletFeed starts, it gets your plan from the App Store. If you have a plan but **Settings** shows **Upgrade**, for example on a new iPhone, you can get WalletFeed Pro again with **Restore Purchases**.

1. In WalletFeed, tap **Settings** (the gear button) at the top of the screen.
2. In the **Subscription** section, tap the **WalletFeed Pro** row that shows **Upgrade**.
3. Tap **Restore Purchases** below the plans.

   If the App Store finds your plan, the **WalletFeed Pro** screen closes. If the App Store finds no plan, WalletFeed shows "No purchases to restore."

   If an error occurs, WalletFeed shows "Couldn't restore purchases."

## Change your plan

You can change from **Yearly** to **Monthly**, or from **Monthly** to **Yearly**. You can also change from a subscription to **Lifetime**. If you have **Lifetime**, you cannot select a subscription.

1. In WalletFeed, tap **Settings** (the gear button) at the top of the screen.
2. In the **Subscription** section, tap **Change Plan**.

   ![The Settings screen with Yearly in the WalletFeed Pro row, and Change Plan and Manage Subscription below it.](app:docs-settings)

   **Settings** closes, and the **WalletFeed Pro** screen opens. Your plan shows **Current**.

3. Tap a different plan.
4. Tap the button at the bottom of the screen, for example **Subscribe** or **Buy Lifetime**.
5. Complete the purchase on the App Store screen.

   The **WalletFeed Pro** screen closes.

If you get **Lifetime** while you have a subscription, the subscription continues until you cancel it. After you get **Lifetime**, WalletFeed does not show **Change Plan** or **Manage Subscription**. To cancel the subscription, use the steps for the Settings app in [Cancel your subscription](#cancel-your-subscription).

## Cancel your subscription

A subscription continues automatically at the end of each period, for example at the end of the free trial or of a month. If you cancel it a minimum of 24 hours before the end of a period, it stops at the end of that period. WalletFeed Pro stays active until that time.

1. In WalletFeed, tap **Settings** (the gear button) at the top of the screen.
2. In the **Subscription** section, tap **Manage Subscription**.

   The subscriptions screen of the App Store opens.

3. Cancel your WalletFeed Pro subscription.

You can also cancel the subscription in the Settings app:

1. Open the Settings app.
2. Tap your name.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.
4. Tap WalletFeed.
5. Cancel the subscription.

## After WalletFeed Pro stops

When WalletFeed Pro stops, WalletFeed keeps your destinations, but it does not send transactions to them. These changes also occur:

- The **Destinations** screen shows **Sync Requires Pro** and an **Upgrade** button.
- If WalletFeed did not send all the history to a destination, the destination screen shows "Paused" below **Sending History**.
- The **Settings** screen does not show the **Sync** section.

The transactions that WalletFeed sent to a destination stay there. The other functions of WalletFeed continue to operate.

## If there is a problem

### If the Destinations screen shows **Sync Requires Pro**

WalletFeed Pro is not active on this iPhone.

1. On the **Destinations** screen, tap **Upgrade**.
2. Tap a plan.
3. Tap the button at the bottom of the screen, for example **Subscribe**.
4. Complete the purchase on the App Store screen.

If you have a plan, you can tap **Restore Purchases** below the plans as an alternative to steps 2 to 4.

### If the WalletFeed Pro screen shows "Couldn't load prices."

WalletFeed cannot get the plans from the App Store.

1. Make sure that your iPhone has a connection to the internet.
2. Tap **Try Again**.

### If WalletFeed shows "Waiting for approval."

The purchase must get approval through Ask to Buy in Family Sharing.

Wait for the approval. After the approval, WalletFeed Pro starts on your iPhone.

### If WalletFeed shows "Couldn't complete the purchase."

The App Store did not complete the purchase.

1. Make sure that your iPhone has a connection to the internet.
2. Tap the button at the bottom of the **WalletFeed Pro** screen again.
