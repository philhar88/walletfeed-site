For an Apple Card year-end summary, Wallet shows your spending for each year and has a statement for each month. The Apple pages in the next section do not name a year-end statement or an annual summary. In WalletFeed, [Tax Pack](/docs/tax-pack/) gives the totals of a year, and [Export CSV](/docs/export-csv/) makes one file of its transactions.

> **Note:** WalletFeed does not give tax advice. The totals are sums of the records in Wallet. They are not a tax form.

## Year-end information from Apple

### Your spending for a year in Wallet

Apple gives these steps in [See your Apple Card spending history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102329):

1. On your iPhone, open the Wallet app.
2. Tap **Apple Card**.
3. Below **Card Balance**, tap the **Monthly Activity** button.
4. Tap **Year**.
5. To show the year before, swipe right.

Below **Total Spending**, Wallet compares the amount with the amount of the period before.

### Statements and a file of the transactions between two dates

Wallet has a statement for each month. Apple gives the steps for a PDF file of a statement in [Download your Apple Card statements or export your transactions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102284). The same page gives these steps for a file of the transactions between two dates:

1. In the Wallet app, tap **Apple Card**.
2. Tap **Card Balance**.
3. Below **Statements**, tap **Export Transactions**.
4. Select a start date and an end date.
5. Select the file format.
6. Tap the done button.

At card.apple.com, **Statements** also has **Export Transactions**. The Apple page does not give the longest period between the two dates.

### Tax documents for Savings

For a Savings account, Apple says: "Tax documents are available by January 31 for customers that have reportable interest." In Wallet, **Tax Documents** is in **Documents** for the Savings account. Apple gives the steps in [View and update your Savings account information in Apple Wallet](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102163).

## Before you start

For the totals and the file in WalletFeed, you must have these items:

- An iPhone with iOS 26 or a newer version. WalletFeed is available only in the US.
- WalletFeed on your iPhone, with [access to your Wallet accounts](/docs/wallet-accounts/). These accounts are Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings.
- For each account, a start date before January 1 of the year. Wallet gives transactions to WalletFeed only from that date.

[WalletFeed Pro](/docs/walletfeed-pro/) is not necessary for the totals on the screen or for **Export CSV**. Some plans include the files of the Tax Pack (refer to [Step 3](#step-3-send-the-files-of-the-tax-pack)).

## Step 1: Find the totals of the year

1. On your iPhone, open WalletFeed.
2. Tap **Settings** (the gear button) at the top of the screen.

   ![The Transactions screen with the gear button at the top.](app:06-transactions)

3. In the **Data** section, tap **Tax Pack**.
4. Tap **Year**.
5. Select the year.

   The screen shows the totals of each Wallet account for that year.

You can also open **Tax Pack** from the **Tax Reports** section, at the end of the **Spending** tab.

The screen shows these totals:

| Account | Totals |
| --- | --- |
| Apple Card | **Purchases**, **Refunds**, **Interest charged**, **Fees** and **Payments to the card** |
| Apple Cash and Savings | **Interest earned**, **Money in, without interest** and **Money out** |

You can select this year or one of the two years before it. Only posted transactions are in the totals. Before the end of a year, the screen shows "The year isn't over. These are the totals so far." For each total, refer to [Totals on the screen](/docs/tax-pack/#totals-on-the-screen).

## Step 2: Make a file of the transactions of the year

1. In WalletFeed, tap **Settings** (the gear button) at the top of the screen.
2. In the **Data** section, tap **Export CSV**.

   ![The Export CSV screen shows This Month in Range, 29 in Transactions and the Export button.](app:docs-export-csv)

3. Tap **Range**.
4. Select **Last Year** or **This Year**.

   **Transactions** shows the number of transactions in the period.

5. Tap **Export**.

   The iOS menu opens with the file, for example `Apple Card Last Year.csv`.

6. Tap **Save to Files**, or select AirDrop, Mail or a different app.

**Last Year** has all the transactions of the year before this year. **This Year** has the transactions from January 1 of this year. Thus, on and after January 1, **Last Year** is the file of the completed year.

The file has one row for each transaction, from each Wallet account that WalletFeed can read. The columns include the date, the merchant, the amount, the category and the account. For all the columns, refer to [Columns in the file](/docs/export-csv/#columns-in-the-file).

## Step 3: Send the files of the Tax Pack

The totals on the screen are free. These plans of [WalletFeed Pro](/docs/walletfeed-pro/) include **Share Pack** and **Share Summary for AI**:

- **Yearly**, but not during its free trial
- **Lifetime**.

With **Monthly**, or during the free trial of **Yearly**, the screen shows **Change Plan**. Without a plan, the screen shows **Upgrade**.

1. In **Tax Pack**, select the year in **Year**.
2. Tap **Share Pack**.

   The iOS menu opens with four files: a PDF summary, a Markdown summary for AI and two CSV files.

3. Tap **Save to Files**, or select AirDrop, Mail or a different app.

The PDF file is a summary of one or two pages. The files have the totals from the screen. They also have the purchases minus the refunds for each category and for each month, and a total for each merchant. For the contents of each file, refer to [Files in the pack](/docs/tax-pack/#files-in-the-pack).

The files go only to the app or person that you select. WalletFeed makes the files on your iPhone and does not keep a copy.

## Get a notification when the year is completed

Tax Pack Ready sends one notification at 9:00 on January 3. Most pending charges of December post before that time. WalletFeed Pro is not necessary for this notification.

1. In WalletFeed, tap **Settings** (the gear button) at the top of the screen.
2. In the **Notifications** section, set the **Tax Pack Ready** switch to on.
3. If iOS shows a message about notifications, tap **Allow**.
4. Tap **Done**.

## Limits of the totals

The totals are sums of the records in Wallet, and they have these limits:

- Pending transactions and transactions in a different currency are not in the totals.
- The date of a transaction, not its posted date, gives the year of the transaction.
- If more than one person uses your Apple Card, the transactions do not show the person who made them.
- Wallet does not identify Daily Cash. Thus, Daily Cash has no total of its own.

[Limits of the totals](/docs/tax-pack/#limits-of-the-totals) gives all the limits. For the totals and the files for your taxes, refer to [Get your Apple Card totals and transactions for your taxes](/guides/apple-card-taxes/).

## If there is a problem

### If the year shows 0 transactions

The year has no posted transactions, or WalletFeed cannot read the transactions of your Wallet accounts.

1. Select a different year in **Year**.
2. If each year shows 0, do the steps in [If each period has 0 transactions](/docs/export-csv/#if-each-period-has-0-transactions).

### If the first months of a year are missing

Wallet gives transactions to WalletFeed from the start date of each account.

1. On your iPhone, open the Settings app.
2. Tap **Privacy & Security** › **Wallet** › **WalletFeed**.
3. For each account, select a start date before January 1 of the year.
4. Open WalletFeed.

### If the Tax Pack screen shows **Upgrade** or **Change Plan**

Your plan does not include the files. The totals on the screen and **Export CSV** stay free.

For the plans, refer to [Plans that include the files](/docs/tax-pack/#plans-that-include-the-files).
