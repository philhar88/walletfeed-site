Support

Email support@walletfeed.app. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version and, for a destination, its type. Never send API keys or transaction details.

Common questions

If your cards don’t appear

Open Settings › Cards › Add Card and choose the accounts to share. Walletfeed reads Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings in the US.

If a destination shows “Authentication failed”

The key or token was revoked or has expired. Open Destinations, tap the destination, then tap Replace Key or Replace Token. Walletfeed keeps the destination’s sync position, so nothing is sent twice.

Sync timing

Transactions arrive within seconds while Walletfeed is open, and within about an hour in the background. File destinations update when you open Walletfeed or a Shortcuts automation runs. To set one up, open Settings › Automations.

Pending charges

Walletfeed sends a pending charge as pending, then updates it in place when it posts. Declined or expired charges are removed from the destination.

Choose which transactions a destination receives

Open the destination and turn on Filter to choose accounts, categories or a minimum amount. With Apple Intelligence, you can also describe a rule, such as “travel over $50, not Uber”.

Change or cancel your plan

In Walletfeed, open Settings › Manage Subscription. You can also open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is on the Walletfeed Pro screen.

Delete your data

Walletfeed has no servers, so it holds nothing about you. Removing a destination deletes its key and sync position from your iPhone. Transactions already sent stay at that service. See the privacy policy.
