Common questions

Walletfeed doesn't show my cards

Open Settings › Cards › Add Card and choose the accounts to share. Walletfeed reads Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings in the US.

A destination says "Authentication failed"

The key was revoked or expired. Open Destinations, tap the destination, then Replace Key. Its sync position is kept, so nothing is sent twice.

How quickly do transactions arrive?

Within seconds while Walletfeed is open, and within about an hour in the background. File destinations update when Walletfeed opens or a Shortcuts automation runs; Settings › Automations shows how to set one up.

What happens to pending charges?

They're sent as pending, then updated in place when they post. Declined or expired charges are removed from the destination.

Can I choose which transactions go where?

Yes. Open a destination and turn on Filter to pick accounts, categories or a minimum amount. With Apple Intelligence, you can also describe a rule in plain language.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In Walletfeed, open Settings › Manage Subscription, or in the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is on the Walletfeed Pro screen.

How do I delete my data?

Walletfeed has no servers, so it holds nothing about you. Removing a destination deletes its key and sync position from your iPhone; data already sent stays at that service. See the privacy policy.
